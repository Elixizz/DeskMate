**You may:**

- Say your project is "based on DeskMate by Lapis Studios" or "a modified version of DeskMate".
- Call your plugin "a DeskMate plugin" or say it's "for DeskMate".
- Use the name to talk about DeskMate, link to it, or review it.

**If you share a modified version of DeskMate, you must:**

- Keep the `Required Notice` copyright line and the LICENSE file.
- State clearly, in the app and wherever you share it, that it's based on DeskMate by Lapis Studios and has been modified.
- Not call it "DeskMate" by itself, and not use the DeskMate icon, so people can tell it apart from the official app.

**You may not:**

- Remove or hide the credit to Lapis Studios.
- Suggest that Lapis Studios made, approved or supports your version or plugin unless we said so.
