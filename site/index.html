Password vault

AES-256 encrypted and stored only on your PC. Built-in 2FA codes with QR import, an optional phone code to unlock, and an optional lock to your Windows account with automatic encrypted backups.

Music

Play your own files and free internet radio, and control YouTube Music, Spotify or anything else playing on your PC, from a player bar and global hotkeys.

Mates for everything else

Mates are small add-ons that get their own tab. They run sandboxed: they can't see your vault, your files or other Mates, and only get what you approve.

The Supply Closet

Verified Mates, reviewed by Lapis Studios. Grab them from the puzzle-piece Mates button → Supply Closet; DeskMate checks every download against the checksum below.

Opening the Supply Closet…

DeskMate 1.0.2 and newer use this store automatically. On an older version, paste https://deskmate.lapistudios.com/plugins/index.json into Plugins → Store.

Build your own

If you can make a web page, you can make a DeskMate Mate: a manifest.json, an HTML page, done. You can share Mates for free or sell them.
